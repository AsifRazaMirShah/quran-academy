Yes. All classes are live and one to one on video call, so you can learn Quran online from any country with a phone or computer.

Yes. Tajweed classes start from Arabic letters and Makharij and move step by step to full rules and fluent recitation.

Do you teach kids and adults?

Yes. We teach children, women and adults, and adjust the pace and style to each student.
